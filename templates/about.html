One application through its full lifecycle

A correctable application stays open. Correct application opens a prefilled form and keeps existing documents unless the vendor replaces them. Submitting creates a new revision under the same application ID and reruns every check. Old revisions remain in the audit trail; they do not count as suspicious repeat attempts.

If a reviewer requests information, the same correction path is used. The vendor sees a safe evidence list, never the reviewer's internal note.

The checks

The process checks completeness, country rules, identifier formats and checksums, document presence/type signature, cross-field identity, legal-name match, demo verification data, duplicates and—with staff visibility only—fresh attempts after terminal rejection.

  • India: GSTIN, company PAN and IFSC.
  • United States: EIN and routing checksum.
  • Germany: VAT check digit, IBAN checksum/length and BIC. VIES is optional.
  • Other countries: never auto-approved; they go to review.

Look-alike substitutions, hidden characters, mixed alphabets, exact sanctions matches, embargoed bank countries, cancelled registration and placeholder tax IDs are terminal hard stops. A single ordinary identity contradiction stays open for correction; two together reject.

Payment-fraud controls

  • An onboarded vendor changing its bank account goes to review.
  • A bank account shared with another vendor goes to review.
  • The server blocks approval of either case until the reviewer records a trusted-contact callback or independent bank confirmation.
  • Bank account numbers are masked in every staff and vendor view.
  • From the fourth linked fresh application after terminal rejections, a reviewer decides even if the latest form is clean.

Reviewer controls and communication

A reviewer must take a case before deciding it; only the holder can decide or release it. Every decision needs a reason. Evidence shows which fields caused the finding and, for existing-vendor cases, what is the same and what differs.

Vendor messages are generated from safe reason and document mappings. Reviewer text, other-vendor data, internal vendor IDs, attempt controls and screening mechanics never appear on the vendor page or in mail. Messages are saved to an outbox, retried, and grouped in one thread per application.

Honest demo boundaries

  • The tax registry and sanctions list are demo fixtures. An unknown registry ID is labelled not checked, not verified or rejected.
  • PDF/PNG/JPG signatures are checked, but document content, authenticity, malware and expiry are not.
  • Automatic bank-account ownership verification is unavailable.
  • This case-study app has no staff login, roles or CSRF protection. SQLite and in-memory progress are single-instance choices.
  • Mail is simulated unless Brevo is configured, and real recipients must be on an allow-list.

Rules are deterministic and versioned (current version {{ rule_version }}); no AI makes an approval decision.
