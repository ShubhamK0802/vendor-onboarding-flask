About

Who uses this

  • Vendors fill in the form and upload documents. They see only the status and what they need to fix.
  • Reviewers use the Reviewer desk: a queue of vendors needing a human, the evidence and documents, and Approve / Reject / Request more info with a required reason.

How a submission is decided

Seven checks run in order: completeness, country routing, format checks, documents, identity checks, name match, duplicate check. Each finding has a level:

  • Level 1: the vendor can fix it (missing document, typo in a format).
  • Level 2: needs human judgment (similar names, shared bank account or contact, a vendor that is already onboarded).
  • Level 3: identity red flag (GSTIN and PAN disagree, tax ID from the wrong country).

Approved only when nothing at all is found. Pending, resubmission required: only Level 1 findings. Pending, in review: any Level 2, or exactly one Level 3. Rejected: two or more Level 3, or one Level 3 plus a missing document.

Anything the system cannot positively verify is never approved automatically: it goes back to the vendor, to a reviewer, or is rejected.

What happens after the verdict

  • A reviewer's decision becomes the vendor's current status; the rules' original verdict is kept next to it.
  • Request more info keeps the run open, waiting for the vendor.
  • A corrected resubmission is linked to the earlier run, which is then marked superseded.
  • Approved vendors join the list the duplicate check compares against. An already-onboarded company that submits again always goes to a reviewer, and a different bank account is called out.

Design choices

  • Fixed rules decide, not an AI. Every outcome can be explained line by line and repeated exactly.
  • Thresholds are our own design (names at least 80% similar go to a reviewer), kept in one config file. Rule version {{ rule_version }}.
  • Nothing is overwritten. Reviewer decisions are separate records next to the original verdict.
  • Bank account numbers are masked everywhere they are shown.
  • The history starts empty. Only the vendors already on file (for the duplicate check) exist before the first run.

Known limits

  • No live checks against tax registries, sanctions lists or bank-account ownership.
  • Documents are stored for the reviewer to open, but their contents are not read. A forged document of the right type would pass.
  • India and US only, one bank account per submission. No expiry tracking and no automatic reminders.
  • Data lives in a SQLite file; on free hosting it can reset on restart.
